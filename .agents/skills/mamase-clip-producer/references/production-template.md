# Mamase 8-Scene Production Template

## Standard Story Arc

All Mamase clips follow an 8-scene structure (7 content scenes + 1 Brand Outro) designed for maximum engagement, clear storytelling, and emotional resonance.

---

## Scene-by-Scene Guide

| Scene | Purpose | Typical Duration | Visual Direction | Narration Goal | Suggested Motion | Suggested Transition |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Hook** | 7–9 sec | Most striking, dramatic, or mysterious image; strong vertical composition | Create intense curiosity immediately without revealing the whole secret | `slow_zoom_in` | `dissolve` |
| **02** | **Context / Location** | 6–8 sec | Wide establishing shot, map-like geography or atmospheric environment | Clarify what, where, or when the subject takes place | `pan_down` | `smooth_left` |
| **03** | **Reveal** | 7–9 sec | Closer distinct view that clearly exposes the mystery/subject | Explain the primary surprising feature or central claim | `pan_left_to_right` | `dissolve` |
| **04** | **Scientific Explanation** | 8–10 sec | Macro, diagrammatic, microscopic, or process-focused visual | Explain the underlying scientific mechanism in clear, accessible Thai | `slow_zoom_in` | `fade_black` |
| **05** | **Evidence / Deep Dive** | 8–10 sec | Documentary evidence, real-world consequence, close-up (avoid gore) | Dispel common myths, add credible evidence, deepen understanding | `slow_zoom_out` | `fade_white` |
| **06** | **Plot Twist** | 8–10 sec | Contrasting, lively, or unexpected visual | Reveal a surprising counter-intuitive fact that reverses assumptions | `pan_right_to_left` | `smooth_right` |
| **07** | **Ending / Reflection** | 6–8 sec | Wide cinematic closing shot (e.g. sunset, vast pullback) | Summarize the awe and wonder of nature/science without repeating hook | `slow_zoom_out` | `dissolve` |
| **08** | **Mamase Brand Outro** | 3 sec | **Fixed Brand Asset**: Blue planet, cyan orbit, midnight navy background | **Locked Narration**: `ค้นพบโลก ค้นพบใจ กับ Mamase` | `slow_zoom_in` | `fade_black` |

---

## Storytelling Best Practices
1. **Hook Strength**: The first 3 seconds are crucial. Start with a provocative question or a mind-bending premise.
2. **Clarity**: Explain scientific mechanisms using simple analogies rather than dense jargon.
3. **Pacing**: Maintain an active rhythm with 6–10 seconds per scene.
4. **Tone**: Inspiring, curious, informative, and respectful (จักรวาลของใจ).
