# Forever Young Story

Status: **draft for channel-owner review**. Until approved, show the proposed
script and Scene 01 key art before producing a public-ready package.

Create Thai TikTok/Reel/Shorts stories for adults who want to remain curious,
healthy, confident, and emotionally alive as they grow older. Treat age with
dignity. Never promise youth, shame visible aging, or sell fear.

Before producing content, read only the references relevant to the task:

- For topic choice, claims, voice, hooks, and CTA, read
  [references/content-bible.md](references/content-bible.md).
- Before creating any image, read
  [references/visual-bible.md](references/visual-bible.md).
- Before writing `script.json` or a ZIP, read
  [references/package-rules.md](references/package-rules.md).

## Required workflow

1. Identify the channel pillar and the single emotional promise of the clip.
2. Fact-check health, psychology, finance, history, and current-event claims
   using primary or authoritative sources. State uncertainty honestly.
3. Draft the complete Thai script and scene plan for review.
4. After approval, create Scene 01 first and use it as the visual anchor.
5. Inspect every image at full size and mobile size; regenerate mismatched,
   generic, distorted, watermarked, or visually inconsistent scenes.
6. Build one ZIP that remains compatible with FFmpeg Motion and Wan: every
   scene keeps an image and `motion`; add `wan` only where requested.
7. End with the approved Forever Young Story outro, never Mamase branding.

Do not copy Mamase's anime presenter, space palette, closing line, logo, or
outro. Do not copy Thai Java Zone's software-explainer style.

## Draft approval gate

The reusable visual assets under `assets/forever_young_story/` are proposals.
Do not treat presenter identity, logo, palette, tagline, or outro as canonical
until the channel owner explicitly approves them. Once approved, update this
status and record the selected asset paths in the visual bible.
