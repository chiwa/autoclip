# Forever Young Story — draft identity for review

This proposal assumes the channel serves Thai adults aged roughly 40–65 with
warm, useful stories about health, fresh starts, relationships, nostalgia, and
credible longevity. Nothing in this folder is canonical until approved.

## Proposed identity

- Promise: growing older can mean becoming freer, wiser, healthier, and more
  interesting.
- Tagline: **อายุเพิ่มได้ แต่หัวใจไม่ต้องหยุดเติบโต**
- Visual style: premium warm cinematic editorial storytelling.
- Palette: sunrise gold, warm cream, deep teal, sage, and plum.
- Voice: mature, natural, conversational, quietly playful.
- People: authentic Thai/Asian adults with real skin texture and agency.

## Review decisions

1. Keep or change the core audience and five content pillars.
2. Approve or revise the tagline and CTA.
3. Approve the palette and editorial-photo direction.
4. Decide whether the channel needs a fixed presenter.
5. Select a Google TTS voice after listening tests.
6. Approve the logo and outro before adding this skill to `start.md` and
   `AGENTS.md` routing.

## Presenter candidates

- Candidate A: warm female storyteller in a home library.
- Candidate B (recommended): active Thai male storyteller, approximately
  48–55, lean and healthy, wearing premium active-smart clothing in a sunlit
  home wellness studio. The image communicates exercise and self-care without
  bodybuilding, supplement sales, or unrealistic age reversal.
