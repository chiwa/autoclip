# Mamase space-reel visual benchmark

Use this brief as the default reference for new space and science Reel imagery.
Open individual Parker images only when a particular composition needs closer
study.

## What makes the Parker look work

- **One heroic idea per frame:** the Sun, spacecraft, heat shield, or Earth is
  large and immediately readable on a phone.
- **Full vertical composition:** visual energy runs from top to bottom. Keep a
  clean lower-center subtitle band, but never leave a half-empty black canvas.
- **Cinematic science:** physically credible hardware and astronomy, dramatic
  directional light, rich warm orange against deep navy/blue, visible texture,
  atmosphere, particles, and scale.
- **Story through composition:** show a relationship or action—probe near the
  Sun, shield against heat, solar energy reaching Earth—not an isolated object
  placed on a generic star field.
- **Clean master art:** scene 2 onward has no labels, diagrams, English text,
  logo, watermark, HUD, or baked subtitle. AutoClip narration/subtitles carry
  the explanation.
- **Hook as key art:** scene 1 unifies presenter, topic title, short hook, and
  the main science subject in the same light and visual world. The presenter
  must feel integrated, not pasted over a background.

## Reject list

Regenerate a scene with large unmotivated empty space, a small/faint subject,
generic starfield filler, infographic styling, embedded labels/logos, flat
lighting, inconsistent visual style, or a picture that does not make the
scene's narration clear before the viewer reads a subtitle.
