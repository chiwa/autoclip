## 1. Technical Compliance & Creative Standards

All 9 vertical image assets have been created and verified according to the channel's strict production rules in `agent.md` and `assets/parker_solar_probe_reel/visual-reference.md`:

1. **Resolution & Aspect Ratio**: Every asset is strictly **1080x1920 PNG** (9:16 vertical), resampled and composed via high-fidelity Lanczos resampling.
2. **Visual Standard (Real Science + Cinematic Documentary)**:
   - Built with authentic high-resolution imagery and spacecraft engineering models from **ESA (European Space Agency)** and astronomical solar physics data from **NASA**.
   - Zero flat programmatic vector drawings, zero cartoonish elements, zero empty black starfield slides.
   - High dynamic range lighting: blazing golden-white solar corona and thermal radiation contrasting with deep inky cosmic space.
   - Active visual composition fills 70–80% of the frame with strong foreground, midground, and background depth.
3. **Subtitle Safe Area**: Lower-center region (y: 1380–1920, x: 160–920) is preserved as a clean, calm, dark zone across all scenes for AutoClip Thai subtitles.
4. **Presenter Fidelity (Scene 01 Only)**:
   - Presenter strictly preserves the canonical Mamase presenter identity from [`assets/characters/mamase-presenter-v1.png`](file:///Users/zengcode/projects/autoclip/assets/characters/mamase-presenter-v1.png) (Thai anime male, tousled black hair, thin rectangular glasses, navy blazer, black shirt, explanatory pointing gesture).
   - Integrated naturally into the scene with directional warm golden rim-light matching the solar glare on his left side and soft ground drop shadow.
   - Only Scene 01 includes the presenter; Scenes 02–08 are 100% pure documentary visuals without any presenter.
5. **Thai Typography (Scene 01 Only)**:
   - Topic Title: `ความลับดาวพุธ` in warm gold `#FFD666` (44pt).
   - Hook: `ทำไมไปดาวพุธ...\nยากกว่าที่คิด?` in crisp white `#FFFFFF` (76pt).
   - Rendered using Sukhumvit Set Bold with Gaussian blurred deep drop shadows at top-left outside subtitle safe area.
   - Scenes 02–08 contain zero text, numbers, infographic cards, HUD, logos, or watermarks.
6. **Mandatory Canonical Outro (Scene 09)**:
   - Exact copy from canonical outro asset (`dist/mamase-james-webb-time-machine-reel-v1/images/scene-11-mamase-outro.png`).
