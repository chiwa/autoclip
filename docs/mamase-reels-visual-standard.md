## 2. The Visual DNA

Every Mamase Reel scene belongs to the exact same premium science-documentary universe:

| Element | Specification & Quality Standard |
|---|---|
| **Tone & Look** | High-end Netflix / NASA streaming documentary frame. Photorealistic, physically believable, richly textured. Never cartoon, anime, generic AI fantasy, children's book illustration, flat infographic, or poster template. |
| **Color Palette** | Deep space navy (`#050810`), cosmic black, stark icy white, subtle atmospheric cyan, accented with restrained warm directional sunlight (gold/orange). |
| **Scientific Hero** | One colossal scientific subject dominating the frame (planet, moon, star, black hole, spacecraft, comet, nebula). Rendered with high texture fidelity, accurate cratering/plasma/ice, rim lighting, and dramatic scale. |
| **Recurring Characters** | Young adult male explorer/storyteller and his fluffy dog companion. Expedition parka, technical backpack (with Mamase emblem/patch), viewed primarily from behind or 3/4 rear angle gazing outward. Vary pose, position, body direction, and gaze from scene to scene while maintaining identical character identity. |
| **Foreground Environment** | Rugged natural terrain (rocky cliffs, icy ridges, regolith, boulders) grounding the camera and establishing immense foreground-to-background depth. |
| **Astrophysical Lighting** | Directional sunlight, rim light on limbs, atmospheric backscatter, authentic stellar reflections. Dynamic phenomena (plasma arcs, geysers, ice fracture, dust venting) only when scientifically factual. |
| **Typography (Scene 01)** | Editorial documentary hierarchy: Large topic title (with organic texture like frost/rock), short Thai headline, short English supporting line, minimal technical line callouts, subtitle-safe lower center. Clean, uncluttered. |
| **Content Scenes (02+)** | 100% clean visual immersion: NO generated text, NO HUDs, NO agency watermarks. Only the locked Mamase corner logo (`assets/branding/mamase/logo.png`) composited via script. |
| **Motion-Ready (Wan)** | Composed for real physical motion (drifting dust, outgassing, rotating rings, boiling flares, fracturing ice, atmospheric flow) rather than basic camera pan/zoom. |
| **Emotional Core** | **“เราเล็กมาก แต่กำลังมองสิ่งที่ยิ่งใหญ่มาก”** *(We are tiny, yet gazing upon the colossal universe)* — wonder, curiosity, existential scale, mystery, and scientific credibility. |
